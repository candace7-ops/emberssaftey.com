Problem

The operational gap is the minutes before an emergency call/Mayday. Heat, exertion, and smoke can stack quickly, while radio contact and visibility are not guaranteed. Earlier warning can change outcomes.

Warns the wearer first

Vibration-only alerts (one button; minimal training).

Rule-based escalation

Auditable (not black-box AI): Normal → Warning → Urgent → SOS only when corroborated risk persists and there is no acknowledgement.

TEAM‑SOS via LoRa

Sends a local TEAM‑SOS to nearby armbands and a crew receiver via LoRa, with optional last-known GNSS/GPS location and age-of-fix.

How it works (simplified)

  • Quick baseline (~1–3 minutes) and offline crew check-in using Shift IDs.
  • Monitors trends from heart-rate (PPG; used only when reading quality is good), skin temperature plus a second pod/ambient temperature sensor, motion pattern, and CO trend.
  • Manual SOS always available; acknowledgement prevents escalation; clear requires deliberate action.
  • No phone, towers, or cloud by default; location is sent only in Urgent/SOS (or check-in).

Scope and limitations

  • Complements PPE and the buddy system; does not replace radio Mayday or emergency traffic.
  • Not a medical device; does not measure core body temperature or diagnose.
  • PCM is passive heat buffering for comfort and sensor-contact stability (not active cooling).
